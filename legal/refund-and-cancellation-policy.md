# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Keystone Digital Sdn. Bhd.** (1694093-U) · Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). Where an order form or partner agreement says something different, that document prevails.

## 1. Subscriptions

1. **Cancel anytime.** You can cancel a subscription from your account or by emailing us. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you keep access until then.
2. **No partial-period refunds.** Fees already paid for the current period are not refunded, except as set out below or required by law.
3. **Annual plans.** If you cancel an annual plan within **14 days** of the first purchase and have not made substantial use of it (more than 10% of included usage), we will give a full refund. After that, annual fees are non-refundable.

## 2. Usage-based charges and credits

1. Usage charges (for example AI tokens, image, video or 3D generations, and API calls) are for resources already consumed and are non-refundable.
2. Prepaid credits are non-refundable and non-transferable, and expire 12 months after purchase unless stated otherwise.
3. If a generation fails because of a fault on our side or our provider's side, we will re-credit the affected usage.

## 3. Professional and one-off services

For incorporation, partner onboarding, setup and other professional services:

1. You may cancel before work starts for a full refund, less any third-party or government fees already paid (for example SSM fees).
2. After work starts, we will charge for work done and costs incurred, and refund any balance.
3. Government fees, third-party fees and filed submissions are non-refundable.

## 4. Our failures

If we materially fail to provide a paid Service and do not fix it within 30 days of your written notice, or if we discontinue a paid Service, you may cancel and receive a pro-rated refund of prepaid fees for the unused period.

## 5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method, or by bank transfer to an account in the payer's name, within **14 business days**. We will issue a credit note through MyInvois where required.

## 6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank. We may suspend accounts with open chargebacks while they are investigated.

## 7. Consumers

Nothing in this policy affects rights that consumers have under the Consumer Protection Act 1999 or the Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transactions) Regulations 2012 that cannot lawfully be excluded.
