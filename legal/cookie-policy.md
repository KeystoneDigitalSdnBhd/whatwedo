# Cookie Policy

**Keystone Digital Sdn. Bhd.** (1694093-U) · Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies (local storage, pixels and SDKs) on our websites and apps. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your actions and preferences, keep you signed in and understand how it is used.

## 2. Cookies we use

| Type | Purpose | Examples | Consent needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strictly necessary** | Sign-in, session security, load balancing, bot and abuse protection | Session cookie, CSRF token, Cloudflare security cookies (e.g. `__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance`) | No, because the Services cannot work without them |
| **Preferences** | Remember language, theme and similar choices | `ks_lang`, `ks_theme` | Yes |
| **Analytics** | Understand usage and improve performance, using aggregated data | Vercel Web Analytics, Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless where possible) | Yes |
| **Marketing** | Measure campaigns and show relevant ads | Meta, Google or TikTok pixels, only if enabled | Yes |

The cookie list may change as the Services evolve. We keep this table up to date.

## 3. Your choices

- When you first visit, we ask for your consent to non-essential cookies. You can accept, reject or customise them, and change your choice at any time from the "Cookie settings" link in the site footer.
- You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Services working.
- To opt out of interest-based advertising, use the settings provided by the relevant ad platforms.
