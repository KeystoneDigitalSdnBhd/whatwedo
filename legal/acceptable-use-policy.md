# Acceptable Use Policy

**Keystone Digital Sdn. Bhd.** (1694093-U) · Effective date: 17 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms). It applies to everyone who uses the Services, including your end users. Our AI model providers' usage policies also apply to content generated through their models.

## 1. Prohibited uses

You must not use the Services to:

1. **Break the law**, including the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998, the Penal Code, the Copyright Act 1987, the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, the Anti-Money Laundering, Anti-Terrorism Financing and Proceeds of Unlawful Activities Act 2001, and sanctions laws.
2. **Exploit or harm children**, including generating, possessing or distributing child sexual abuse material. We report such material to the authorities.
3. **Create non-consensual intimate or sexual imagery** of real people, or deepfakes intended to deceive, defame, harass or impersonate.
4. **Commit fraud or deception**, including phishing, scams, fake reviews, fake invoices or e-invoices, forged identity or corporate documents, or misleading people into thinking they are dealing with a human when they are not, where that matters.
5. **Infringe intellectual property** or other rights, including generating content designed to copy a specific artist's or brand's protected work, or removing watermarks or provenance markers.
6. **Harass, threaten or incite** violence, hatred or discrimination, including on the basis of race, religion or royalty (the "3R" issues) in breach of Malaysian law.
7. **Spread disinformation** intended to manipulate elections or public opinion, or that endangers public health or safety.
8. **Develop weapons or cause serious harm**, including chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear or explosive weapons.
9. **Attack systems**, including malware, unauthorised access, vulnerability exploitation without authorisation, or denial-of-service.
10. **Process sensitive personal data** or carry out profiling, surveillance or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals without a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.
11. **Provide regulated advice** (legal, medical, financial) to others without appropriate human professional oversight.
12. **Gamble, or offer unlicensed financial services**, through our payment and banking-rail tools.

## 2. Platform integrity

You must not:

1. Access the Services by automated means except through our documented APIs, or exceed rate limits or quotas.
2. Share, sell or sublicense API keys or accounts, or resell the Services without a written partner agreement.
3. Reverse engineer the Services or use them, or their Outputs, to build a competing model or service, where prohibited by us or our model providers.
4. Bypass safety filters, content moderation or usage limits, including through prompt-injection techniques ("jailbreaking").
5. Misuse device configuration (MDM) profiles, including installing them on devices you are not authorised to manage.

## 3. Disclosing AI-generated content

Where it is reasonably expected, or legally required, clearly label content generated by AI, especially realistic images, video or audio of people or events.

## 4. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected breaches, remove content, and suspend or terminate access without notice where necessary to prevent harm or comply with law. We may report unlawful activity to the authorities and cooperate with them.
