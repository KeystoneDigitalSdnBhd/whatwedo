## Before publishing

These are drafts prepared for Malaysian law (PDPA 2010 as amended in 2024, Contracts Act 1950, Consumer Protection Act 1999, Electronic Commerce Act 2006). **Have them reviewed by a Malaysian lawyer before relying on them.** Search for `TO CONFIRM` and fill in:

- Legal, DPO, abuse and billing contact emails
- The Data Protection Officer's name (and register the DPO with the PDP Commissioner)
- Pricing-specific refund terms, if they differ from the defaults
- The sub-processor list, checked against vendors actually in use
- The cookie list, checked against what the live website sets
